Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-23

This policy applies to the personal OAuth client ted assist gws, a single-user command-line tool (gws CLI) used by the account owner to access their own Google Account data (Google Calendar and Gmail).

What the app accesses

  • Google Calendar events, used to read and manage the owner's own schedule.
  • Gmail, used to read and send the owner's own email.

How data is handled

  • The app is used by exactly one person: the account owner.
  • Data is processed locally on the owner's computer and is not sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies are present on this website or in the app.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored locally in an encrypted credential store and can be revoked at any time in Google Account security settings.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's data access changes. The current version is always published at this URL.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the developer contact email listed on the OAuth consent screen for this project.
